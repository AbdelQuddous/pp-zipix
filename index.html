Zipix Privacy Policy

App: Zipix: Compress Photo to KB · Package: com.abdelquddos.zipix · Developer: AbdelQuddos · Effective date: 27 November 2026

In short: Zipix compresses your photos and videos entirely on your phone. Your files are never uploaded, there is no account, and the developer does not collect your personal information. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects limited device data to serve and measure ads.

1. Who we are

Zipix ("the app") is an Android app developed by AbdelQuddos ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies only to the Zipix app.

2. Your photos and videos

  • All compression, resizing and conversion happens locally on your device.
  • The photos and videos you pick are read from your device, processed in memory and in the app's private cache, and saved back to your device.
  • They are never uploaded, sent, shared or stored on any server, by us or by any third party, and they are never used for advertising.
  • Results are saved to the Pictures/Zipix or Movies/Zipix folder on your device, or shared only to the app you choose in the Android share sheet.
  • Temporary working files in the app cache are removed by the app and by Android; they never leave the device.
  • Compression works without an internet connection.

3. Information we do not collect

Zipix has no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or the contents of your files. We do not run our own analytics or servers.

4. Information stored only on your device

  • History: recent results (file names, sizes before and after, date). You can delete it at any time in the app.
  • Settings and Pro status: your preferences and whether Pro is unlocked.

This data stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the app.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings.
  • Buying Pro removes ads.
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

6. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Zipix Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google; we never receive your card or payment information. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own Pro.

7. Ratings

The app may show Google Play's in-app review prompt. It is optional, and we receive no personal data from it.

9. Data sharing, retention and security

  • We do not sell your data and we do not share your files with anyone.
  • Data collected by Google for ads is governed by Google's policies linked above.
  • Data that travels to Google services is encrypted in transit.
  • Because your files stay on your device, they are protected by your device's own security.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Since we do not hold personal data about you, you can control everything directly: delete history in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it, manage ad consent in the app, and reset your advertising ID in Android settings. For data collected by Google, use Google's privacy controls.

11. Children

Zipix is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with the effective date at the top.
